📖  How It Works — The Credit Stacking Strategy
What We Do
What is Business Credit Stacking?
Business credit stacking is the strategy of opening multiple business credit accounts at the same time — before banks share reporting — to maximize your approved credit limits without any single lender seeing the others. The result? Hundreds of thousands of dollars in available capital, often at 0% interest for 12–18 months.
